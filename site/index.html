Questions sellers ask us

Is there any cost to me?

No. There are no commissions or fees, and we pay the normal closing costs. The offer we agree on is what you receive.

How fast will I get an offer?

Usually within 24 to 48 hours. If we need a detail like the parcel number, we'll call you.

Can I just call instead?

Of course. Call or text (980) 990-2356 and you'll reach Damien.

The land has back taxes, liens or several heirs. Can you still buy it?

Very often, yes. We work through these with the closing attorney all the time and will tell you honestly what it takes.

How do I get paid?

The closing attorney pays you by wire or check once everything is signed and recorded.

What do you do with the land after?

We may hold it, resell it to a builder or a family, or help get something built on it.
