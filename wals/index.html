Hi, I'm Damien Dupee.

I didn't come into land with money or connections. I was delivering pizza and driving 50+ hour weeks when I started teaching myself how vacant land deals work. I had to push through a lot of adversity, learn it on my own, and keep going when deals fell apart.

Since 2016 I've run Dominion Land Group, buying off-market vacant land in the Carolinas and selling lots to builders. This course is the exact process I use: how I find sellers, what I say, how I price, the contracts I sign, and how I find the buyer.

If you're willing to put in the work, I'll show you every step.

What your first 90 days look like

Plan on 2–4 hours a day. Here's the work, month by month. How fast you get a deal depends on your market, your marketing budget, and how consistently you follow up.

Month 1

Learn the basics, pick your market, pull your first list, and set up your phone, texting, and CRM.

Month 2

Market to sellers by mail or text, run comps, make offers, and start building your buyers list.

Month 3

Work your follow-ups, get a lot under contract, and market it to builders and investors.

Questions

Can I start with little or no real estate experience?

Yes. The course starts with the basics and builds to advanced strategies. No license or prior experience is needed to follow it.

What do I need to begin?

A phone, a computer, some money for marketing (mail or texting), and a coachable attitude.

How long do I have access?

For life, including future updates to the course.

What's the difference between the course and coaching?

The course is self-paced video training with the scripts, templates, and contracts. Coaching is six weekly 1-on-1 calls with me where we work your actual deals, comps, and pricing. Compare them here.
